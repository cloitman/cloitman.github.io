SunStrong Solar Battery Optimization

Monetization modelling for residential storage systems

January 2026 - May 2026 • SunStrong Management

Overview

Modelling monetization and VPP opportunities for residential storage systems. SunStrong Management is an asset manager with approximately 500,000 residential solar systems leased to homes. By identifying revenue streams such as policy incentives, time-of-use (TOU) arbitrage, and virtual power plant (VPP) opportunities, they can appropriately invest in building residential storage systems.

Price Forecasting Pipeline

Building a price forecasting pipeline against ERCOT real-time market data. This involves ingesting historical and real-time pricing data from the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) market to predict future price movements and identify optimal battery dispatch schedules.

Revenue Streams

The analysis identifies and quantifies multiple revenue streams for residential battery storage:

  • Policy incentives and utility rebate programs
  • Time-of-use arbitrage: charging during low-price periods and discharging during peak pricing
  • VPP aggregation: coordinating distributed batteries to provide grid services
  • Demand response participation
  • Capacity market revenues

Impact

The modeling work helps SunStrong evaluate the business case for adding battery storage to their existing solar installations, informing investment decisions across their portfolio.
