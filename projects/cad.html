December 2020 - Present

Summary

I like to design useful objects for around my home. Here are some of my more complicated projects.

Corkscrew

I was tired of pulling a cork straight out of a bottle, potentially resulting in spillage. I decided to 3D print my own using high in-fill to both hold the bottle of wine and remove the cork in one motion. I designed a second-class lever system which serves to multiply force making the bottle open easily.

Nut Crusher

I wanted to mock up a nut crusher. The five-bar mechanism serves to multiply force at the expense of distance. The outside is made using laser cut epoxy resin with 3D printed parts. Held together using steel rods and O-rings. The motor was too weak to crack nuts.
